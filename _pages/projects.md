## Selected Projects

Here are some selected projects I developed focused on public health and accessibility.

---

### HeartPulse IQ

A PPG-based cardiovascular monitoring web app that uses a smartphone camera to estimate heart rate and analyze rhythm patterns live. It incorporates an AI-based health coach, Google account log-in, and long-term tracking of health records.
[App](https://heartpulseiq.streamlit.app/)

---

### StrokePulse

A machine learning–driven web app to assess stroke risk from user-inputted health records and facial images.
[App](https://strokepulse.streamlit.app)
